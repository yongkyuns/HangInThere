HDR video imports use libraries from FFmpeg 8.1.3 under the GNU Lesser General Public License version 2.1 or later (LGPLv2.1+), and zimg 3.0.6 under the WTFPL version 2.

FFmpeg: https://ffmpeg.org
zimg: https://github.com/sekrit-twc/zimg

Exact unmodified source archives accompany the Android CI APK as the android-hdr-source artifact. Sources, checksums and reproduction instructions: https://github.com/yongkyuns/HangInThere/blob/codex/android-parity/docs/ANDROID-HDR.md

FFmpeg libraries are dynamically linked. This application imposes no restriction on replacing those libraries or reverse engineering for debugging such modifications. The complete JNI wrapper and build recipe are available in the same repository. No GPL or nonfree FFmpeg components are enabled.
